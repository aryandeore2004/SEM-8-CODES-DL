Q14. What does confusion matrix show?

👉 TP, TN, FP, FN values.

Q15. What is precision?

👉 TP / (TP + FP)

Q16. What is recall?

👉 TP / (TP + FN)

Q17. What is F1-score?

👉 Harmonic mean of precision and recall
